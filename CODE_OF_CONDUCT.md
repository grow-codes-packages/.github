# Code of Conduct

Grow Codes Packages adopts the Contributor Covenant 3.0. It applies in our community spaces and when participants officially represent the community. [Contributor Covenant 3.0][covenant]

We expect participants to welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience, give constructive feedback, respect boundaries and privacy, and help repair harm when it occurs. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing private information without consent, and disruption of community spaces are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [opensource@grow.codes](mailto:opensource@grow.codes). Include the relevant community space, what happened, and any context you can safely share. Maintainers handle reports discreetly and use the Contributor Covenant's guidance to choose a response proportionate to the conduct, which may include a correction, a warning, or removal from community spaces.
